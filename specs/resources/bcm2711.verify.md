  high-peripheral premise is gone and the claim is scoped to Low Peripheral mode. `armstubs/` at

  and "armstub.S and armstub7.S are 32-bit" hold. The TF-A page ("TF-A port design") says the
  firmware, finding a magic value in the stub, puts the two load addresses in memory near the
  beginning of memory, and that the stub is loaded at address 0; "writes ... into it" reads
  that together with the stub's own `dtb_ptr32`/`kernel_entry32` words (201-208), which the
  sentence then attributes to the source. The magic-slot clearing is now attributed to the
  comment at armstub8.S:190-192, which says it. Addresses: ARM Local 0xFF80_0000 and GIC base
  0xFF84_0000 in Low Peripheral mode (BCM2711 Peripherals §6.5.1–6.5.2, p. 92); distributor at
  0x1000–0x1FFF and CPU interfaces at 0x2000–0x3FFF (GIC-400 TRM §3.2, Table 3-1, p. 3-3);
  so the stub's 0xFF80_0000, 0xFF84_1000 and 0xFF84_2000 (37, 49-50) are the Low Peripheral
  ones. The `armstub` and `arm_64bit` premises match config_txt/boot.adoc, and `enable_gic`
  matches the legacy page. The only conditionals in armstub8.S are `BCM2711`, `HIGH_PERI` and
  `GIC` (32, 33, 45, 53, 133, 212). The build-identity assumption is stated as one and the TODO
  is present. Contrary-evidence glance: nothing at the pin contradicts the Low Peripheral
  default build.

  RES1 on p. D24-9640. The stated consequences match.
- Quick-facts/4 "SCR_EL3 value": PASS — premises are the six bits of 59-66; 0x400 + 0x100 + 0x80

- Quick-facts/8 "Secondary-core release: spin-table": PASS — MPIDR bits 1:0 select the path

  build-identity step, which the overlay's preamble already states.
- Quick-facts/9 "GIC programming at EL3": PASS — the register offsets and `IT_NR` = 8 (85-89);
  `setup_gic` is called before the `eret` under `GIC` (133-135); in `setup_gic` (214-235) a core
  with non-zero MPIDR bits 1:0 writes 3 to `GICD_CTLR`, core 0 branches past it under a "primary
  core" comment, and every core writes 0x1E7 to `GICC_CTLR`, 0xFF to `GICC_PMR`, and all-ones to

  0xFF84_1000/0xFF84_2000 (45-51). "Every core" is supported by the source: secondaries reach the
  spin loop only after the `eret`, which follows `setup_gic`.

  from-reset condition is stated as an assumption ("assuming the cores reach the stub from
  reset"). §D8.2.12.1 rule RPPDBS (p. D8-7730): with stage 1 disabled, an access from Secure

  good") is gone. The bullet now claims only that no SMC, IRQ, FIQ or SError reaches EL3, that

  and above with the `HCR_EL2.TSC` trap taking priority (pp. D24-9639–D24-9640), and EA, FIQ,
  IRQ at 0 keep external aborts, SErrors, FIQs and IRQs below EL3 from being taken to EL3

  `MDCR_EL3` anywhere in the file, which is wider than the 91-148 anchor but re-established
  over the whole file at the pin. Secondaries leave only through a non-zero slot (151-161);
  booting.rst names spin-table and psci as the enable methods; the TF-A page has BL31 patching
  the DT to advertise PSCI. The derivation follows. Its "only spin-table remains" also assumes
  nothing at EL2 below the kernel offers PSCI over HVC, which the stub's direct `eret` to the
  kernel's EL2 entry makes the case for this boot; not worth a change. TODO present.
- Gotchas/2 "The group settings the stub wrote are secure state": PASS — the retitled lead-in

  datasheet's two maps. Under `HIGH_PERI` `LOCAL_CONTROL` is 0x4_C000_0000 and `GIC_DISTB`
